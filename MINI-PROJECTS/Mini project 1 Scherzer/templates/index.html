      About this form

      A dataset is the result of how it was collected (Mimi Onuoha). This form asks for the truth, then scores people by how far they sit from its idea of normal, and cleanses the ones it finds improbable (Hito Steyerl). The Average Person is built only from entries the machine approved. Nobody is that person.
